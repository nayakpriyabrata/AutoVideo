## WHAT "LOVED BY ALL" MEANS — My Scoring Rubric

Every name below scored 0-10 on 6 axes (60 max):

1. **Short & Spellable** — Can a 10-yr-old type after hearing once?
2. **Global Pronounce** — Odia kid + Mumbai auto + US viewer all say same?
3. **Positive Emotion** — Does it feel love/peace/bliss/shelter instantly?
4. **Cross-Religion** — Hindu/Muslim/Sikh/Christian/secular all welcome?
5. **Future Brand** — Works as Channel + App + Merch + Studio + Cafe + NGO?
6. **Ownability** — Likely free @handle without numbers? No mega-channel owning it?

Score >45 = Excellent. 40-45 = Strong. <40 = Risky.
