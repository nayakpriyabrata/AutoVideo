🎞️ FLOW / VEO CLIPS FOLDER
=======================
How to use your Gemini subscription here ($0 extra):

1. In the app, open the "Flow Shot List" tab, enter your topic, click Generate.
2. Open Google Flow (labs.google/fx) -> Text to Video (Veo).
   Set 9:16 portrait (Shorts) or 16:9 (YouTube), 8 seconds per clip.
3. Paste each shot prompt -> Generate -> Download.
4. Rename files to shot00.mp4, shot01.mp4, shot02.mp4... and put them HERE.
5. In the app: Visual style = "My Flow/Veo clips" -> Generate Video.
   The platform assembles everything: voiceover, word captions, music, transitions.

Tip: fewer clips than scenes is fine - clips are reused smartly.
Tip 2: also works with clips from Pika / Kling / Runway / any MP4, MOV, WEBM.
